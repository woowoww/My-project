## Episode intent

A pear who ruins her sketch finds one small, wobbly line she can actually draw, and that line is enough to begin again.

## Bible and banned-content check

- No health, money, or guaranteed-outcome claims in any file. "Tomorrow, she'll draw another" states an intention, not an outcome.

- Tone is warm and hopeful throughout. The one dark note (scene 6 edge darkening and "heavier" light) is removed.
