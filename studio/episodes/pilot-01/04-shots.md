## Shot-level prompt fragments

Each fragment is written to be combined with the scene prompt in `03-scenes.md`. Each is under 40 words.

**Shot 1 (Medium, static):**
> Vertical 9:16 medium shot, eye level, static. Pip the pear sits beside a tipped ultramarine paint jar and a ruined sketch on worn oak. Soft golden light from camera-left. Open space above and below.

**Shot 2 (Close-up, static):**
> Vertical 9:16 close-up, eye level, static. Pip's sage face with honey-gold cheeks glances down at the ruined page, then lifts her eyes to camera. Soft golden light from camera-left. Warm, quiet.

**Shot 3 (Wide, static):**
> Vertical 9:16 wide shot, static. Pip sits small on the edge of a worn oak table, hands in her lap, shoulders low, plain cream wall behind. A blank sheet lies out of reach. Warm golden light from camera-left.

**Shot 4 (Close-up, static):**
> Vertical 9:16 close-up, eye level, static. Pip's face, shoulders low, eyes on the dried blue page. Long, patient stillness with one slow breath. Faint honey-gold blush. Golden light from camera-left.

**Shot 5 (Medium close-up, slow push-in):**
> Vertical 9:16 medium close-up, slow push-in. Pip's sage hand and raised pencil hover over a fresh cream sheet, one crooked graphite stroke on the page. Tense, still, golden light from camera-left, slate shadow at table edge.

**Shot 6 (Close-up, static):**
> Vertical 9:16 close-up, eye level, static. Pip freezes mid-breath, pencil still in the air beyond frame, small body tense, eyes on the crooked line. Faint honey-gold blush. Golden light from camera-left.

**Shot 7 (Medium, static):**
> Vertical 9:16 medium shot, static. Pip sets a large paper face-down at the side of the oak table, then picks up a fresh pencil with her other hand. Calmer golden light from camera-left. Quiet, steady.

**Shot 8 (Macro, static):**
> Macro close-up, static, top-down. A fresh pencil tip touches the blank cream page at one small point, leaving a tiny graphite dot. Warm oak grain, clean cream paper, soft golden light from camera-left. Hopeful, still.

**Shot 9 (Macro, static):**
> Macro close-up, static, top-down. One slightly shaky graphite line crosses the cream page from left to right, wobbly and uneven. Visible paper fibres, warm oak edge, soft golden light from camera-left. Gentle, no dramatic lighting.

**Shot 10 (Close-up, slow push-in):**
> Vertical 9:16 close-up, slow push-in. Pip looks down at her line, mouth softening into a small smile, honey-gold blush warming on her cheeks. Soft, still golden light from camera-left. Warm, hopeful.

**Shot 11 (Wide, static):**
> Vertical 9:16 wide shot, static. Pip stays in frame beside the cream sheet, turned partly away, cheek visible, not leaving. The sheet holds one wobbly graphite line. Plain wall, lower amber sun from camera-left. Calm, hopeful.

**Shot 12 (Medium, static):**
> Vertical 9:16 medium shot, static. Small cream sheet with one wobbly graphite line in the foreground. Pip's near cheek and honey-gold blush clearly readable at the right third, catching amber light. Clear open space above for a text card.

## Flags for the Director

1. **Final frame conflict.** Scene 6 prompt has Pip turning away from the table, but scene 6 and card 8 need her honey-gold blush visible in the closing frame. Shot 11 has her leaving and shot 12 has the blush at the frame edge. Decide whether Pip stays in frame or whether the blush is shown by a separate sheet-and-cheek shot.
2. **Beat 3 has no VO line** (per `02-story.md` flag 1). Shots 5–6 carry it visually. If the Writer adds a line, timing may need to change.
3. **Card reading time.** Eight cards are placed across shots 1–12. The current timings do not allot extra time for reading cards. The Director may need to extend shots 1, 3, or 12 to fit card holds.
4. **Movement choices.** Push-ins on shots 5 and 10 only. Shot 5 starts the tension and shot 10 is the emotional peak. Both follow the story change. Director can remove either.
