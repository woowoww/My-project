- Target loudness: about -14 LUFS integrated for the full mix. VO peaks at or below -1 dBTP.

This package maps cards 1–2 to shots 1–2, card 3 to shot 7, card 4 to shot 8, card 5 to shot 9, and cards 6–8 to shots 11–12. Confirm, or name a different mapping.

5. **Beat 6 pace.** The 22-word closing VO runs about 2.4 words per second in a 9-second window (29–38s). Confirm this is acceptable, or allow the Writer to trim a line. Any trim is a creative change and needs the Writer and Story Writer.
