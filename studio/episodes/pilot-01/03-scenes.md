# pilot-01: Starting over. Scenes

Source: `02-story.md` (beats 1–6), `01-writing.md` (voiceover and cards)
Character: Pip the Pear (see `studio/bible.md`)
Format: vertical 9:16. Keep clear space above and below the subject for text cards. No text is generated inside the video.

## Look and light (applies to every scene)

- **Location:** one room, one table. A worn oak kitchen table by a window. Nothing else changes location.
- **Time of day:** Tuesday late afternoon. Beats 1–5 use golden light from a window on the left of frame. Beat 6 is the same light, lower and amber.
- **Light source (continuity):** the window, camera-left. Soft, warm, no hard shadows. Slate shadows pool at the table edge.
- **Background:** plain plaster wall, out of focus, warm cream to pale slate. No clutter behind Pip.
- **Mood:** warm, quiet, hopeful. No dramatic lighting, no dark vignette.

## Shared scene palette

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm cream paper | `#F4EEDD` | Paper, wall light |
| Worn oak | `#A9774B` | Table, props |
| Late-light slate | `#5C6B73` | Shadows, background wall |
| Ultramarine | `#2F4FA3` | Paint accent only (jar and spill) |
| Graphite | `#3A3F44` | Pencil line only |

Light colour (not a palette swatch): warm golden window light, amber at beat 6.

**Pip's signature colour:** soft sage green `#9CAF88`, with honey-gold blush `#E0A526` on the cheeks only. Honey-gold is reserved for Pip's blush in every scene, so it always reads as her.

## Props

One strong prop: **the sheet of cream paper.** It carries the whole story (ruined, crooked, one line).

Supporting props, each used in one or two scenes only:
- **Paint jar** (ultramarine): tipped in beat 1, upright after that, with a blue stain on the table edge.
- **Pencil** (wood, graphite tip): slips in beat 3, a fresh pencil in beat 4.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Setup (beat 1, 0–6s)
- **Location:** oak table by the window.
- **Time of day:** late afternoon.
- **Props:** the ruined pencil sketch (paint-soaked, ultramarine spill across it), tipped paint jar, pencil on the table.
- **Background:** plain cream wall, out of focus.
- **Lighting mood:** warm golden, soft.
- **Palette:** cream `#F4EEDD`, oak `#A9774B`, slate `#5C6B73`, ultramarine `#2F4FA3`. Pip: sage `#9CAF88` with honey-gold blush.
- **Prompt:**
  > Vertical 9:16. A small, slightly lopsided pear named Pip, sage green with a honey-gold blush, sits on the edge of a worn oak table in late afternoon. Pip looks down at the sketch, then turns toward the camera. Warm, quiet mood. Open space above and below for text. No text in frame.

### Scene 2: Struggle (beat 2, 6–12s)
- **Location:** same table edge.
- **Time of day:** late afternoon.
- **Props:** the ruined sketch, now with dried blue paint. Jar upright. Two blank sheets sit at the far side of the table, out of reach. Scene 3 takes the top sheet for the outline; scene 4 takes the second, smaller sheet.
- **Background:** plain cream wall, out of focus.
- **Lighting mood:** still and warm.
- **Palette:** cream `#F4EEDD`, oak `#A9774B`, slate `#5C6B73`, ultramarine `#2F4FA3` (dried on the page). Pip: sage with honey-gold blush.

  > Vertical 9:16. Pip the pear sits on the edge of the oak table, hands in her lap, shoulders low, gazing at a page with dried blue paint. Late afternoon, golden window light from the left. A fresh blank sheet lies out of reach across the table. Still, patient mood, no motion except a slow breath. Muted sage and oak tones with a faint honey-gold blush. Open space above and below for text. No text in frame.

No text in frame.

No text in frame.

## Continuity notes

- **Window light:** always camera-left. Golden in beats 1–5, amber in beat 6.
- **Table and wall:** same oak table and plain cream wall in all six scenes. No change of room.
- **The sheet:** the ruined sketch (beats 1–2) is replaced by a fresh sheet (beat 3). The big outline (beat 3) is set face-down in beat 4 and stays face-down. The small sheet with the one line (beats 4–6) is the closing prop.
- **Paint jar:** tipped in beat 1, upright in beats 2–6. The blue stain stays on the table edge as a quiet reminder.
- **Pencil:** the slip in beat 3 is the same pencil that Pip sets down in beat 6. Beat 4 uses a fresh pencil, so the pencil is a new one from beat 4 on.
- **Colour rule:** ultramarine appears only in the paint jar and its spill (beats 1–2). Honey-gold appears only as Pip's blush. Graphite appears only in pencil lines (beats 3, 5, 6).
- **Pip's blush:** present in every scene, warmest in beat 5 and visible in beat 6.
