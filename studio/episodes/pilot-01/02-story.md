# pilot-01: Starting over. Story beats

Theme: starting over
Runtime target: 30–40 seconds, 9:16
Source: `01-writing.md` (voiceover and cards)

## Character

- **Name:** Pip
- **Fruit:** Pear (small, slightly lopsided, round-bellied). Sage green with a warm honey-gold blush.
- **Want:** To finish one small thing she started, just once, all the way through.
- **Fear:** That starting again means the first try was already a failure.

## Beats

| # | Beat | Temperature | Approx. time | Voiceover / cards | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setup | Tender | 0–6s | "Pip knocked the paint over on Tuesday. The sketch was ruined." Cards 1–2 | Hook: Pip beside a tipped paint jar and a ruined sketch. She looks at it, then at the camera. |
| 2 | Struggle | Tense | 6–12s | "She sat on the edge of the table for a long time." | Pip on the table edge, paint still drying on the page. The blank sheet stays out of reach. |
| 3 | Small failure | Tense | 12–17s | No VO line (see flag 1) | Pip pulls a fresh sheet and starts a big, careful outline. The pencil slips and the first stroke goes crooked. She stops with the pencil in the air. |
| 4 | Turn | Hopeful | 17–23s | "Then she thought, okay. Not the whole picture. Just one line." Cards 3–4 | Pip sets the big outline down, takes a fresh pencil, and touches the page at one point. |
| 5 | Quiet win | Hopeful | 23–29s | "So she drew one line. It was wobbly. It was enough." Card 5 | One slow, slightly shaky line across the page. Pip looks at it and her blush warms. No music swell. |
| 6 | Closing line | Hopeful | 29–40s | "Tomorrow, she'll draw another. Starting over doesn't have to be big. It can be one line. One small line is a beginning." Cards 6–8 | Pip leaves the sheet on the table with the one line visible. Final frame holds card 8. |

Total VO runs about 30 seconds. Card holds and beat 3 fill the rest to 35–40 seconds.

## Closing line (final frame)

**"One small line is a beginning."**

Hold on screen for 2–3 seconds (card 8). Visual is Pip's single line on the page, with the honey-gold blush in frame.

## Flags for the Writer

1. **Beat 3 has no voiceover line.** The VO goes from "sat for a long time" straight to "Then she thought, okay." The small failure is visual only (the crooked first stroke). The Writer should decide whether to add a short line or keep it silent. I have not changed any VO line.
2. **Card 7 paraphrases the VO.** Card 7 says "Starting over can be small." The VO says "Starting over doesn't have to be big." The meaning matches, so I am flagging it only so the wording can be checked.
3. **Hook option 3 and the caption add content not in the VO.** "Pip's plan lasted four minutes" and "Some days, small is the whole plan" do not appear in the voiceover. They are not contradictions, but the Story Writer's beats do not use them.

No other mismatches. The VO's "wobbly" and "enough" lines address Pip's fear directly: a wobbly first line is still a start, not a failure. The want (finishing one small thing) is carried by the line she completes in beat 5.
