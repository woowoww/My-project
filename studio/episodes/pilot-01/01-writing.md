# pilot-01: Starting over

Theme: starting over (after a setback, beginning again, a small first step)
Character: Pip the Pear (see `studio/bible.md`)

## Hook options (first 2 seconds)

1. **Visual gag:** Pip sits beside a tipped-over paint jar and a ruined sketch. She looks at it, then looks at the camera.
2. **Question:** "What if the bravest thing today is one small line?"
3. **Line:** "Pip's plan lasted four minutes. Her next one is smaller."

## Voiceover script (30–45 seconds)

Pip knocked the paint over on Tuesday.
The sketch was ruined.
She sat on the edge of the table for a long time.

Then she thought, okay.
Not the whole picture.
Just one line.

So she drew one line.
It was wobbly.
It was enough.

Tomorrow, she'll draw another.
Starting over doesn't have to be big.
It can be one line.

One small line is a beginning.

## On-screen text cards (max 8 words each)

1. Knocked over? Start again.
2. Pip's sketch was ruined.
3. Okay. Not the whole picture.
4. Just one line.
5. Wobbly is still a start.
6. Tomorrow, one more line.

8. One small line is a beginning.

Card 8 is the closing line. Hold it on screen for 2–3 seconds.
