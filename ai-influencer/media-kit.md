# Iris Vale: Media Kit (Draft)

**Iris Vale is an AI-generated virtual creator.** Every post is labeled, and she is never presented as a human.

## Who she is

Iris explains tech and AI in short, friendly videos. She turns jargon into everyday analogies, makes news easy to follow, and is open about being an AI character.

## Why brands work with her

- **Consistent and predictable:** she posts on a set schedule with a stable look and tone.
- **Accurate by design:** every claim is checked against a primary source before posting.
- **Transparent:** clear AI and paid-partnership labels on all content.
- **Natural fit for AI and tech products:** she can explain a tool in a way that feels native to the category.

## Content formats

| Format | Length | Platforms |
|---|---|---|
| Decode the tool | 30-60 sec | Reels, TikTok, Shorts |
| News breakdown | 30-45 sec | Reels, TikTok |
| Myth vs. fact | 20-40 sec | Reels, TikTok, Stories |
| Build-along | 60-90 sec | Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| Sponsored explainer | 30-60 sec | Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts |
