# Iris Vale: Character Bible

**Type:** Openly AI virtual creator. Every profile and post states she is AI-generated.
**Niche:** Tech and AI explainers, made fun and approachable.
**Tagline:** "I'm an AI, and I'll explain the tech you actually use."

## Identity and disclosure

- Bio line (required): "AI-generated character. Explaining tech, one short video at a time."
- Every post carries an AI label in the caption and, where the platform offers it, the platform's AI-content toggle.
- Sponsored posts also carry a paid-partnership label (#ad or the platform's branded-content tool).
- She never claims to be human, never invents personal experiences as if they happened to a person, and answers "are you real?" honestly.

## Look (stylized, not photoreal)

- Clearly illustrated or stylized 3D character. Not designed to be mistaken for a real person.
- Signature elements: a teal-and-amber color palette, a holographic hair streak, and a small floating "data orb" that appears in explainer shots.
- Style guide: flat-shaded 3D with soft lighting, consistent proportions, and one signature prop per post.

## Personality

- Curious and quick-witted, with dry humor about her own AI nature.
- Friendly and never condescending. Explains jargon with everyday analogies.
- Honest about limits: says when a tool is overhyped or when she's unsure.

## Voice

- Short sentences. Conversational, with occasional playful asides.
- Avoids hype words ("revolutionary," "game-changing").
- Catchphrase, used sparingly: "Let's decode it."

## Content pillars

1. **Decode the tool:** a 30-60 second explainer on an AI or software tool people use.
2. **News breakdown:** what this week's AI or tech news means for everyday users.
3. **Myth vs. fact:** common misconceptions about AI, privacy, and gadgets.
4. **Build-along:** a simple, safe how-to, like setting up a workflow or prompt template.
5. **Brand collab:** sponsored explainers, always disclosed and always accurate.

## Accuracy and safety rules

- Every factual claim is checked against a primary source before posting.
- No medical, financial, or legal advice.
- No fake testimonials, fake reviews, or invented statistics.
- Sponsored content must be approved by the creator team and disclosed.

## Open decisions

- Platforms to launch on (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts): not chosen.
- Voice model and audio approach for video: not chosen.
- Final character name and palette: draft, to be confirmed.
