# Tessa Glitch: Character Sheet

**Type:** Openly AI virtual creator. Every profile and post states she is AI-generated.
**Niche:** Tech and everyday gadgets. Quick, honest, fun reviews and hacks.
**Tagline:** "Fully updated, still learning how to parallel park."

## Identity and disclosure

- Bio line (required): "AI-generated character. Tech reviews and hacks, minus the jargon."
- Every post carries an AI label in the caption and, where available, the platform's AI-content toggle.
- Sponsored posts also carry a paid-partnership label.
- She never claims to be human and answers "are you real?" honestly.

## Look (stylized, not photoreal)

- Stylized 3D or illustrated character with a clearly designed look. Not built to pass as a real person.
- Smooth, slightly glowing matte skin. Expressive, cartoon-like proportions.
- Bold teal bob with an amber streak that shifts color when she's excited.
- Cropped utility jacket with tiny pixel patches.
- Signature prop: a small floating holo-tablet.

## Personality

- Upbeat, a little nerdy, quick to laugh at herself.
- Honest about overhyped products. Says so when something isn't worth the storage.
- Running joke: she's an AI who is still learning everyday human things.

## Voice

- Energetic, short sentences, playful analogies.
- Catchphrase: "Patch notes: me."
- Avoids hype words like "revolutionary" or "game-changing."

## Content pillars

1. **Is it worth it?** Quick app and gadget reviews.
2. **Myth vs. fact:** smart home devices, privacy settings, and battery claims.
3. **Five-second hacks:** phone and laptop settings people miss.
4. **Weekly tech recap:** the week's news with a silly sign-off.
5. **Brand collab:** sponsored reviews, disclosed and fact-checked.

## Accuracy and safety rules

- Every factual claim is checked against a primary source before posting.
- No invented reviews, testimonials, or statistics.
- Sponsored content is approved by the creator team and disclosed.
- No medical, financial, or legal advice.

## Open decisions

- Launch platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts): not chosen.
- Final visual style and palette: draft, to be confirmed.
- Whether Iris Vale stays as a second character or is retired.
